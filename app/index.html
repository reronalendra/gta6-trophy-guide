    Sources & honesty note

    Every trophy's official name, description and grade comes from the official PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on all 55. Each guide is written in our own words for this guide; where a fact comes from a published guide — the 200-hour estimate, the 4/10 difficulty, the no-mission-replay rule, the one missable meditation — the guide names that source beside the trophy. A few official descriptions are deliberately vague (a joke, or a hidden moment); where the exact trigger is still being confirmed by guide writers, the guide says so instead of inventing a location or a step.
